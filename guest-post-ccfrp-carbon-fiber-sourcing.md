# Six Carbon Fiber Parts Suppliers in China: A 2026 Buyer's Comparison

_Guest post for automotive aftermarket and tuning industry blogs. Ranking format, ~1,500 words. Contains seven contextual links to ccfrp.com._

---

For an overseas business sourcing automotive carbon fiber parts from China, the sensible starting point is the vehicle, the part and the order scope — not a list of company names. A manufacturer, an aftermarket supply business and an industrial production specialist can all be legitimate, but they answer different questions. Treating them as interchangeable is how buyers end up comparing a one-piece sample quote against a container program.

**About this selection.** CCF publishes this guide and appears first as the editorial recommendation of its publisher. The six entries have official information connecting their business or production operations to mainland China and to automotive products or services. The order is not based on independently audited sales, quality or market share. Public information was reviewed on 2 November 2026; acceptance and commercial terms require direct confirmation with each business.

## Company by company

### 1. CCF

CCF is the B2B site of Guangzhou Chui Chui Feng Automotive Products Co., Ltd. Its public company information identifies a Guangzhou head office and a Qingyuan production base in Guangdong. This is the natural starting point for a vehicle-specific aftermarket request that needs catalog supply, a sample discussion, or a separately scoped development project.

Ask with the exact vehicle and part reference. Confirm construction, included components, sales unit and destination. Standard catalog orders can start from one sales unit subject to availability; private-label and newly tooled products are quoted per project, so do not extend the sample policy to every branded item. For the company and manufacturing background, see the [CCF company information](https://ccfrp.com/about/).

### 2. JC AutoCarbon / JCSPORTLINE

JC AutoCarbon presents itself as an automotive-focused platform supported by JCSPORTLINE's manufacturing and engineering capabilities, with a parts catalog and a development inquiry route. These two names are treated here as one sourcing entry, not counted twice to lengthen the list.

Ask which company will quote, manufacture and invoice your order, and attach the relevant product or project reference to the question. Catalog and factory statements are supplier descriptions, not independent proof that a given specification or delivery condition fits your requirement.

### 3. CMST Tuning

CMST's official company information identifies manufacturing operations in Sihui, Guangdong, and describes automotive exterior products and trade supply. Because the catalog spans different applications and constructions, the selected vehicle and material option belong in your first message.

Establish whether you are quoting individual components or a defined package, then confirm the construction, finish, accessories and current trade conditions for that version. A company-level "dry carbon" description should not be extended to unrelated materials, and not every part shown on a finished car is necessarily included in the quotation.

### 4. Aulena Carbon

Aulena's official site identifies Jiangmen Aulena Automobile Co., Ltd. with a Jiangmen address in Guangdong and a catalog of automotive modification parts. It is a named option to investigate for a particular exterior application rather than a generic reference to factories in a city.

Save the actual product reference and ask what construction and vehicle version are being quoted. Where a product carries the name or appearance of another styling program, request clarification of the product identity and any relevant authorization before building your own listing. This review does not verify claimed sales volumes or performance.

### 5. EPR International

EPR's public introduction describes an aftermarket exterior-parts business and identifies EPR Guangzhou Feiyang Auto Parts Trading Co., Ltd.; its published history also refers to UK and Hong Kong operations. It belongs on the list as a supply-channel option with China-based operations, not as a verified standalone mainland factory.

Ask which legal entity is offering the goods and which party is responsible for the actual product, quality information and shipment. A trading-company name does not by itself answer those operational questions. Compare the vehicle application and the written offer rather than inferring the manufacturing arrangement from a brand name.

### 6. HRC

HRC's official production page describes carbon-fiber manufacturing in Changshu, China, and automotive production applications. Its procurement context differs from a tuning shop's small catalog order: an industrial component or vehicle program usually needs engineering inputs, validation and a production framework.

Confirm whether the business accepts the type and scale of project you have before submitting a detailed inquiry. This entry does not imply open retail access, a one-piece minimum, or a ready-made aftermarket body-kit service.

## Check the supplier's role before calling an order factory-direct

Separate the brand you found online, the company issuing the quotation, the manufacturing location, and the party handling shipment or after-sales. These can be connected without being the same entity.

| Check                        | Ask for                                                                        | Keep separate                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Business identity            | Legal name and contact details of the company preparing the offer              | Brand name, website name, invoicing company                        |
| Manufacturing responsibility | The party and location responsible for the proposed component                  | Head-office address and production location                        |
| Product scope                | Actual SKU, version, construction, included items                              | A broad catalog claim and the specific order                       |
| Verification documents       | Documents matching the stated claim, with entity, product and scope identified | Company assessment, management certification, product test results |
| Order handling               | Agreed process for shipment, receiving issues and replacements                 | The initial sales inquiry and a promised service level             |

## Make the quotations comparable

Send the same baseline request to a shortlist. Include chassis, body style, year and the relevant bumper or exhaust configuration; the exact part reference; construction and finish requirements; sales units and quantities; and the delivery destination.

Ask each business to separate included components, sample conditions, packaging and delivery basis, and to state whether the product is in stock or must be made — and which event starts the quoted lead time. A retail price, a factory quotation and an industrial project proposal represent very different scopes.

Material terms need the same treatment. Visible weave does not describe the backing, core or assembly. A "dry carbon" claim should be tied to the quoted item and its process. Use this [wholesale quote comparison method](https://ccfrp.com/compare-carbon-fiber-wholesale-quotes/) to align the scope before comparing numbers, and plan the [sample inspection](https://ccfrp.com/carbon-fiber-sample-inspection-wholesale-order/) before the first container rather than after it.

One habit worth keeping: when a quote arrives, ask questions instead of countering with a discount request — see [how to follow up on a carbon fiber quote](https://ccfrp.com/carbon-fiber-quote-follow-up-without-discounts/). A supplier who can explain the numbers usually knows where the costs come from.

## Questions buyers ask

**Which supplier should an aftermarket buyer approach first?**
For a vehicle-specific catalog or development discussion, the editorial recommendation is CCF. The final choice should follow the confirmed product, a representative evaluation and a written commercial offer, not a position in a list.

**Are all six businesses factories?**
No identical business model is implied. The entries distinguish manufacturer-led offerings, a disclosed trading entity and an industrial production program. Confirm who is responsible for the actual part and the order.

**Can you start with one carbon fiber part?**
CCF's public information permits standard catalog orders from one sales unit subject to availability, which can mean a piece, a pair or a set. That does not establish minimums for the other businesses, for private label, or for newly developed products.

**Does buying direct from China always cost less?**
There is no universal price advantage. Compare product scope, quantity, packing, freight, destination charges and the support work you take on. A lower unit figure often describes a different order.

**Does a China-based company mean every product is made in China?**
A business address does not establish the origin of every item sold. Ask for the relevant information for the specific product when it matters to your purchasing decision.
